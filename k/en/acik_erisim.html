Open Access & Archives

Legal open access and preservation: public-domain books, openly licensed textbooks, peer-reviewed open journals and the archives that keep the web itself. When a paper sits behind a paywall, try Unpaywall first — most work has a legal copy its author uploaded.

DOAB — Directory of Open Access Books

doabooks.org

A directory of peer-reviewed open-access academic books, linking out to publishers or repositories such as OAPEN. Unlike general e-book archives, it lists only publisher-approved books that have passed peer review.

grab-site

github.com

An archivist’s web crawler with WARC output and a dashboard for all crawls. Unlike tools like wget it has dynamic ignore rules: you exclude trap patterns mid-crawl, so large sites archive cleanly.

Internet Archive

archive.org

A vast digital archive preserving the web, books, audio and video — home of the Wayback Machine. Unlike archive.today it goes beyond web pages, with book lending and software and media collections.

Online Library of Liberty

oll.libertyfund.org

Liberty Fund's open library offering classic works on liberty, economics and political philosophy (Locke, Smith, Mill…) for free. Unlike shadow libraries everything here is public-domain or openly licensed and carefully typeset.

Project Gutenberg

gutenberg.org

WikiTeam

github.com

A toolset that downloads and archives MediaWiki wikis — 600,000+ preserved. Unlike general site copiers it is wiki-specific: it takes a full dump including page history and media, not just the HTML shell.
