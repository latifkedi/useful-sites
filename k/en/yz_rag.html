AI · RAG, Embeddings & Vectors

On the retrieval side the decisions that matter are in the pipeline rather than the model: how you chunk documents, which embedding model you pick, whether you rerank. Choosing a vector database is usually the least consequential call — pgvector carries small projects, and running a separate system only pays for itself past a certain volume.
