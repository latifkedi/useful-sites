Testing & QA

Two separate needs: a stable target to practise automation against, and an API that answers requests realistically. Sauce Demo and The Internet serve the first, JSONPlaceholder and Restful-Booker the second. The deliberate misbehaviours are intentional — they teach you the trap, not that your test is broken.
