Economics

In economics the real distance is between data and interpretation: World Bank, IMF and FRED give the raw series, while RePEc and NBER give the work arguing what explains them. Before reading a chart, check the unit and the period — nominal or real, per capita or total.

25 links

BIS — Bank for International Settlements

bis.org

The central bank of central banks; publishes global financial statistics and research papers.

Academic, Reference, Free

EconJobMarket

econjobmarket.org

A platform managing academic economics job applications in one place, collecting applications and reference letters. A standard application flow instead of scattered emails.

Reference, Free

EconLib

econlib.org

A library holding an economics encyclopedia, essays and full texts of classic economics works.

Reference, Book, Free, Academic

Economics Stack Exchange

economics.stackexchange.com

An economics Q&A site where you can ask experts about concepts and models. Unlike a forum, voting and editing surface the best answer.

Free, Interactive, Reference

FRED

fred.stlouisfed.org

The St. Louis Fed’s economic database — 800,000+ US and world series, with charting and an API. Unlike raw statistical releases, the browse-plot-download flow lives in one place.

Data Science, Free, Reference

IDEAS / RePEc

ideas.repec.org

The largest database of economics publications — over two million papers, working papers and software items. Unlike Google Scholar it is field-specific: it indexes economics alone and derives author and institution rankings.

Academic, Reference, Free

IGM Experts Panel

igmchicago.org

A panel where leading economists vote whether they agree with topical policy questions. Unlike a single expert’s view it shows at a glance where the consensus sits.

Academic, Free

IMF Data

imf.org

The IMF’s macro data — the standard reference for balance of payments, exchange rates and debt. It leans more to monetary and fiscal statistics than the World Bank.

Data Science, Free, Reference

JOE · Job Openings for Economists

aeaweb.org

The American Economic Association’s official job board — the central place for the academic economics market. Unlike general job sites it is economics positions only.

Reference, Free

Khan Academy · Ekonomi

khanacademy.org

A free video-and-exercise set teaching the basics of micro, macro and finance from scratch. Unlike university courses it targets the introductory level and intuition.

Free, Interactive, Curriculum

Marginal Revolution

marginalrevolution.com

Tyler Cowen and Alex Tabarrok’s daily economics blog — among the most-read in the field. Against news it offers a short, frequent stream of commentary through an economist’s lens.

Free, Reference

MIT OCW · İktisat

ocw.mit.edu

Over 100 courses across every major field of economics in MIT’s open courseware. Unlike YouTube playlists these are full course structures with prerequisites and reading lists.

Curriculum, Free, Academic

NBER Working Papers

nber.org

Working papers from the US National Bureau of Economic Research — most later appear in peer-reviewed journals. You read the field’s leading research here before the journals.

Academic, Free, Reference

OECD Data

data.oecd.org

Official OECD statistics across member economies, standardised for cross-country comparison straight from the source.

Data Science, Reference, Free, API

OpenStax · Principles of Economics

openstax.org

A free, openly licensed textbook for introductory university economics. Unlike commercial texts it is free and regularly updated, with PDF, web and print editions.

Book, Free, Academic

Our World in Data

ourworldindata.org

University-backed research publication presenting long-run data on the economy, population, health and environment as interactive charts.

Data Science, Reference, Free, Interactive, Academic

QuantEcon

quantecon.org

Open-source lectures and libraries for computational economics (Python and Julia). Unlike pure theory it teaches building and solving models in code — the de facto standard for quantitative economics.

Open Source, Python, Free

RePEc

repec.org

A network of services for economics researchers: bibliography, working-paper feeds, blog aggregator. IDEAS is its front end; repec.org is the volunteer infrastructure behind it.

Academic, Reference, Free

RFE · Resources for Economists

aeaweb.org

A guide of thousands of links for economists, maintained by the American Economic Association. A directory curated by the field itself rather than random search.

Reference, Free, Awesome List

SSRN Economics

ssrn.com

A preprint archive of economics working papers. Unlike NBER it is not tied to one institution: anyone can upload, so it is broader but less filtered.

Academic, Free

Statista

statista.com

A statistics portal aggregating market and survey data across industries with sourcing.

Data Science, Reference, Freemium

Trading Economics

tradingeconomics.com

Gathers macro indicators — GDP, inflation, rates — for 196 countries in one dashboard with historical series and forecasts.

Data Science, Reference, Freemium, API

Visual Capitalist

visualcapitalist.com

A publication turning economic and technology data into infographics. Not a raw data source — its contribution is making magnitudes comparable.

Free, Data Science

VoxEU / CEPR

cepr.org

A stream of columns where researchers explain their own work to a policy audience (CEPR). Unlike a working paper it drops the jargon and makes the finding accessible.

Academic, Free, Reference

World Bank Data

data.worldbank.org

International macroeconomic time series, country by country. Unlike individual statistics offices it is comparable: hundreds of indicators under common definitions in one place.

Data Science, Free, Reference
