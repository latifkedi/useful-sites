Economics

In economics the real distance is between data and interpretation: World Bank, IMF and FRED give the raw series, while RePEc and NBER give the work arguing what explains them. Before reading a chart, check the unit and the period — nominal or real, per capita or total.

Atlas of Economic Complexityatlas.hks.harvard.edu

The Harvard Growth Lab’s atlas visualising a country’s export structure and economic complexity. Unlike raw trade data it comes with derived measures such as the complexity index, which reads growth potential off the export basket.

2
BIS — Bank for International Settlementsbis.org

The central bank of central banks, publishing global financial statistics and research papers. Unlike market-data sites it focuses on systemic and cross-border banking data.

Academic · Reference · Free
3
Bureau of Economic Analysisbea.gov

The official US publisher of GDP, national accounts, personal income and international trade data. Unlike BLS it focuses on production and income accounts rather than the labour market; it has GDP tables by state and industry.

Data Science · API · Free · Dataset
4
Bureau of Labor Statisticsbls.gov

The official US source for employment, unemployment, wages and consumer inflation (CPI). It publishes many series you see on FRED; unlike FRED it goes down to occupation, industry and region breakdowns. It has a public API.

Data Science · API · Free · Dataset

EconJobMarketeconjobmarket.org

A platform managing academic economics job applications in one place, collecting applications and reference letters. A standard application flow instead of scattered emails.

Reference · Free

EconLibeconlib.org

A library holding an economics encyclopedia, essays and full texts of classic economics works. Unlike current data portals it offers concepts and theory rather than data, with a distinctly free-market editorial stance.

Reference · Book · Free · Academic
7
Economics Stack Exchangeeconomics.stackexchange.com

An economics Q&A site where you can ask experts about concepts and models. Unlike a forum, voting and editing surface the best answer.

Free · Interactive · Reference

FREDfred.stlouisfed.org

The St. Louis Fed’s economic database — 800,000+ US and world series, with charting and an API. Unlike raw statistical releases, the browse-plot-download flow lives in one place.

Data Science · Free · Reference · Dataset
9
IDEAS / RePEcideas.repec.org

The largest database of economics publications — over two million papers, working papers and software items. Unlike Google Scholar it is field-specific: it indexes economics alone and derives author and institution rankings.

Academic · Reference · Free

IGM Experts Paneligmchicago.org

A panel where leading economists vote whether they agree with topical policy questions. Unlike a single expert’s view it shows at a glance where the consensus sits.

IMF Dataimf.org

The IMF’s macro data — the standard reference for balance of payments, exchange rates and debt. It leans more to monetary and fiscal statistics than the World Bank.

A free video-and-exercise set teaching the basics of micro, macro and finance from scratch. Unlike university courses it targets the introductory level and intuition.

NBER Datanber.org

Datasets released by NBER researchers: macroeconomic history, CPS extracts, patent data. Unlike NBER Working Papers it is the data behind the papers, not the papers themselves, which makes it useful for reproducible economics.

Working papers from the US National Bureau of Economic Research — most later appear in peer-reviewed journals. Unlike the journals it lets you read leading research months before publication; bear in mind they have not yet been peer-reviewed.

Official OECD statistics across member economies, standardised for cross-country comparison straight from the source. Unlike general data aggregators the data is standardised and comes directly from the official source.

A free, openly licensed textbook for introductory university economics. Unlike commercial texts it is free and regularly updated, with PDF, web and print editions.
