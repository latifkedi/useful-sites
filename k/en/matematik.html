Mathematics

In mathematics the bottleneck is usually intuition, not access: a source that gives the formula and one that shows the picture do very different jobs. 3Blue1Brown and Seeing Theory bring the visual, Paul’s Notes and OpenIntro the ordered course, and math-as-code turns notation into code.

29 links

3Blue1Brown

3blue1brown.com

Grant Sanderson’s channel and site explaining mathematics through animation — linear algebra, calculus, analysis. Unlike lectures it shows the picture behind the formula, not just the formula.

Video, Free, Interactive

abstractmath.org

abstractmath.org

A resource opening the places people get stuck moving into abstract mathematics — reading definitions, the language of proof, notation. Unlike topic books it targets how mathematics is thought.

Tutorial, Free, Reference

Art of Problem Solving

artofproblemsolving.com

Olympiad-level competition-math courses and a forum community.

Tutorial, Academic, Freemium

Awesome Math

github.com

A list organising mathematics resources by branch (algebra, analysis, topology). Unlike a single course it maps out where to start.

Awesome List, GitHub, Reference

BetterExplained

betterexplained.com

A set of essays explaining maths by intuition rather than memorisation — exponentials, Euler’s formula, calculus. Unlike formal treatments it answers “why is it like this” with analogy and pictures.

Tutorial, Free

Desmos

desmos.com

A free online graphing calculator producing fast, shareable interactive graphs.

In Browser, Interactive, Free

Encyclopedia of Mathematics

encyclopediaofmath.org

An EMS/Springer-backed maths wiki of authoritative survey articles.

Reference, Academic, Free

Fundamentals of Linear Algebra (UBC)

math.ubc.ca

A free UBC text building linear algebra from the ground up. Unlike short notes it is a complete, proof-carrying resource for those who want the reasoning behind the course.

Book, Free, Academic

GeoGebra

geogebra.org

Dynamic maths software that links geometry, algebra and calculus live.

In Browser, Interactive, Free

Graph Online

graphonline.ru

Draw a graph in the browser and run shortest path, connectivity and flow algorithms on it — testing graph algorithms without writing code.

Free, Algorithms

Interactive Mathematics

intmath.com

A site teaching mathematics topic by topic with interactive graphs and worked examples. Unlike static notes you can move the graph in place and see the result.

Interactive, Free, Tutorial

Khan Academy · Matematik

khanacademy.org

A free platform teaching mathematics from primary school to university, with exercises in order. Unlike scattered videos it offers a full curriculum that builds on prior topics.

Free, Interactive, Curriculum

Manim Community

manim.community

The community edition of the Python library behind the mathematical animations in 3Blue1Brown videos; you describe the scene in code. Unlike general animation tools it treats equations, graphs and transformations as first-class objects.

Python, Open Source, Image Gen, Free

Math ∩ Programming

jeremykun.com

Jeremy Kun’s blog joining mathematics with working code — cryptography, graphs, the maths of machine learning. Unlike pure theory it closes each concept with an implementation.

Tutorial, Free, Algorithms

math-as-code

github.com

A cheat sheet mapping mathematical notation to readable code: sigma sums, dot products. Unlike symbol glossaries it shows what the formula means to a programmer.

Reference, GitHub, Cheat Sheet

Math3ma

math3ma.com

An approachable graduate-maths blog that builds intuition for abstract topics.

Tutorial, Academic, Free

Mathematics Stack Exchange

math.stackexchange.com

A mathematics Q&A site where you can ask experts about the step you are stuck on. Unlike a forum, voting surfaces the clearest solution.

Free, Interactive, Reference

Mathigon

mathigon.org

An interactive maths textbook with manipulable, story-driven lessons.

Interactive, Tutorial, Free

MathOverflow

mathoverflow.net

A high-rigour question-and-answer site for research mathematicians.

Reference, Academic, Free

MIT · Mathematics for Computer Science

ocw.mit.edu

An MIT course gathering the discrete mathematics a computer scientist needs — logic, counting, graphs, probability — in one place. Unlike general maths courses it aims straight at CS.

Curriculum, Free, Academic

OEIS

oeis.org

The Online Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences — look any sequence up by its terms.

Reference, Academic, Free

OpenIntro Statistics

openintro.org

A free, open textbook for introductory statistics, covering data, inference and regression by example. Unlike commercial texts it is free and comes with exercises and slides.

Book, Free, Academic

Paul’s Online Math Notes

tutorial.math.lamar.edu

Clean, worked lecture notes for algebra, calculus and differential equations (Lamar University). Unlike a textbook it is ideal for a quick refresh before an exam.

Tutorial, Free, Reference

Probability Cheatsheet

wzchen.com

A cheat sheet compressing a probability course’s distributions, expectations and theorems onto one page. Unlike a textbook it can be scanned at a glance during an exam.

Cheat Sheet, Free, Reference

ProofWiki

proofwiki.org

A collaborative maths database holding proofs, not just statements.

Reference, Academic, Open Source

Seeing Theory

seeing-theory.brown.edu

A Brown University project teaching probability and statistics entirely through interactive visuals. Unlike a textbook you see the concept by dragging and trying — you move the distribution by hand.

Interactive, Free, Data Science

Stanford CS229 · Linear Algebra Review

cs229.stanford.edu

A Stanford reference summarising the linear algebra needed for machine learning. Unlike a full course it is a quick refresher focused only on the parts ML uses.

Reference, Free, Data Science

Symbolab

symbolab.com

An equation solver that shows the worked steps to a solution, derivatives, integrals and limits included. Unlike general computation engines it centres on the steps rather than the answer; some detailed steps are paid.

In Browser, Tutorial, Freemium

Wolfram MathWorld

mathworld.wolfram.com

An extensive, rigorous and cross-linked mathematics encyclopedia.

Reference, Academic, Free
