Architecture & Building

The building-architecture field: on one side the publications where you see the project (ArchDaily, Dezeen, Divisare), on the other the tools you draw it with. Among the tools the split is clear — SketchUp is for fast massing, Rhino handles free form, and Revit and Archicad unite drawings and data in one BIM model.
