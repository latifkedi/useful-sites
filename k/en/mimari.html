Architecture & Building

The building-architecture field: on one side the publications where you see the project (ArchDaily, Dezeen, Divisare), on the other the tools you draw it with. Among the tools the split is clear — SketchUp is for fast massing, Rhino handles free form, and Revit and Archicad unite drawings and data in one BIM model.

23 links

Arch2O

arch2o.com

An architecture site giving competitions, software and learning content alongside project news. Unlike a pure project archive it also carries guides aimed at students and young architects.

Reference, Tutorial, Free

ArchDaily

archdaily.com

The world’s largest architecture publication — daily projects, drawings and material news. Unlike Pinterest-style boards it presents each project with plans, sections and the architect’s statement.

Reference, Image Gen, Free

Archello

archello.com

An architecture and design platform gathering projects, products and brands in one place.

Reference, Free

ArchEyes

archeyes.com

An archive examining modern architecture classics in depth with drawings, plans and context.

Reference, Academic, Free

Archicad

graphisoft.com

An architect-focused BIM software rivalling Revit. Unlike Revit its interface is built around the architectural design flow — leaning to design more than engineering modules.

CAD, 3D, Paid, Desktop

Archinect

archinect.com

A long-running community site gathering architecture news, jobs and discussion.

Reference, Free

Architizer

architizer.com

A platform indexing architecture projects alongside building products. Unlike pure inspiration sites it also links the real products and manufacturers used in a project.

Reference, Image Gen, Freemium

Autodesk Revit

autodesk.com

The industry standard for Building Information Modelling (BIM) — model, drawings and data in one file. Unlike pure drafting tools, a wall you place in plan updates automatically in section and the schedule.

CAD, 3D, Paid, Desktop

Designboom

designboom.com

A long-established online magazine covering architecture, design and art broadly.

Reference, Image Gen, Free

DETAIL

detail.de

A reference magazine focused on construction detail and building solutions. Unlike inspiration sites it goes into “how it is built” — junctions, layers and material detail.

Reference, Academic, Paid

Dezeen

dezeen.com

A magazine covering architecture and design with a journalistic voice. Unlike ArchDaily’s project archive it leans on news, interviews and criticism.

Reference, Image Gen, Free

Divisare

divisare.com

An archive presenting architecture through photography alone, slow and selective. Unlike fast feeds it curates each project like a careful photo series.

Reference, Image Gen, Freemium

Domus

domusweb.it

The archive of the authoritative Italian magazine on architecture and design, running since 1928.

Reference, Free

Enscape

enscape3d.com

A real-time visualisation tool embedded inside Revit, Rhino and SketchUp. Unlike separate render programs it gives a photorealistic view instantly while you design, with no export.

3D, Image Gen, Paid

MIT OCW · Mimarlık

ocw.mit.edu

The open courseware of MIT’s architecture department — from design studio to building technology. Unlike publication sites it gives the profession as an academic curriculum, course by course.

Curriculum, Free, Academic

Morphocode

morphocode.com

Articles and tutorials teaching urban data, mapping and generative design.

Tutorial, Reference, Free

Parametric Architecture

parametric-architecture.com

A publication focused on parametric and computational design and AI in architecture.

Reference, Image Gen, Free

Pritzker Prize

pritzkerprize.com

The official site of the Pritzker Prize, architecture’s highest honour — laureates and citations. Unlike general lists it gives the field’s canon year by year, with the jury’s reasoning.

Reference, Free

Rhinoceros 3D

rhino3d.com

A free-form (NURBS) modelling tool; with the Grasshopper plugin, the hub of algorithmic design. Unlike box modellers it handles complex curved geometry and parametric setups.

3D, CAD, Paid, Desktop

RIBA

architecture.com

The Royal Institute of British Architects’ site — professional standards, education and resources. Unlike publication sites it represents the institutional, official side of the profession.

Reference, Academic, Freemium

SketchUp

sketchup.com

A fast 3D modelling tool for architecture and interiors. Unlike heavy CAD/BIM programs it is easy to learn — for turning an idea into massing in minutes.

3D, CAD, Freemium, Desktop

The Architectural Review

architectural-review.com

A long-standing British journal treating architecture with critical and theoretical depth.

Reference, Academic, Freemium

World-Architects

world-architects.com

An international directory presenting profiles and projects of selected architects country by country.

Reference, Free
