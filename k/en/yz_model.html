AI · Models & Assistants

Three separate decisions: which model, where it runs, and where the data goes. Closed APIs give the best performance but your prompt leaves your machine; open-weight models run on your own hardware at the cost of setup and VRAM.

Runs embedded in Word, Excel and the Windows shell, so you operate on a document without copying it to a website. The underlying models are OpenAI’s; the difference is the delivery point.

Builds a context limited to the PDFs, docs and links you upload, and refuses to answer outside them. The constraint cuts hallucination; the price is that you cannot ask it general questions.
